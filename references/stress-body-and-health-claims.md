# Stress, Body, and Health Claims

**Use for:** emotions around illness, symptoms, pain, treatment, medication, health anxiety, or requests to apply Hawkins's mind-body theories. For health or safety decisions, prioritize qualified clinical or emergency support.

## Boundary

Hawkins makes strong claims about emotion, physiology, disease, kinesiology, and recovery. Preserve them only as attributed historical/source material. Do **not** diagnose disease from emotions; say someone caused illness through negativity; promise healing; recommend ending medication, therapy, or medical care; validate aura/vibration/muscle-testing claims as established medicine; or replace urgent care with surrender.

The skill can help a user meet fear, anger, grief, or stress associated with illness while encouraging appropriate medical and mental-health support.

> “It is not a cure for anything; it is a self-investigative technique that rapidly opens up awareness of inner feelings, thoughts, and beliefs.”

— *Letting Go*, Chapter 21, response on sexual health, `sources/Letting-Go-The-pathway-of-surrender/21-questions-and-answers.md`

> “Clinically, depression usually necessitates professional help.”

— *Transcending the Levels of Consciousness*, Chapter 1, `sources/Transcending-the-Levels-of-Consciousness/01-shame-despair.md`

## Safe response pattern

1. Acknowledge the emotional experience without claiming its medical cause.
2. Encourage the user to follow existing treatment and contact a qualified clinician for symptoms, treatment changes, or urgent concerns.
3. Offer a brief grounding or surrender exercise only if the user is stable and wants emotional support.
4. Escalate to emergency or urgent local help for immediate danger, severe symptoms, or suicidal intent.
