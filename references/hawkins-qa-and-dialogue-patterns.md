## Derived dialogue pattern

Use a short sequence, one question at a time: normalize the feeling; ask where it is felt; invite temporary allowance; ask whether resistance or a payoff is present; offer willingness; then ask what reality-based action remains. A response should feel like accompaniment, not interrogation. Avoid “should,” faux certainty, and a sermon about consciousness levels.
