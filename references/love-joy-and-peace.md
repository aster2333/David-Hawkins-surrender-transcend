# Love, Joy, and Peace

**Use for:** users who explicitly want Hawkins's teaching on higher states, compassion, forgiveness, or peace after active emotional work. Do not prescribe love or peace as a shortcut around grief, anger, or danger.

## Practical use

In ordinary guidance, love may mean a little less self-attack, concern for another without abandoning oneself, or an ability to see a person as human while holding a firm boundary. Peace may be a moment of nonresistance rather than a permanent spiritual state. Use the user’s own spiritual language; offer theistic language only when it fits.

> “A way of being that is forgiving, nurturing, and supportive.”

— *Letting Go*, Chapter 3, “The Scale of Emotions,” `sources/Letting-Go-The-pathway-of-surrender/03-the-anatomy-of-emotions.md`

**Source note.** The same chapter describes Peace as a rare, advanced state in Hawkins's framework. Do not claim it on behalf of a user. A more credible close is: “What becomes possible if you do not fight this moment?”

For disidentification, surrender of personal will, or advanced spiritual interpretation, select the dedicated topic page directly from `SKILL.md`; do not follow a chain of topic pages.
