## The Problem of Paradigm

Because the mind, by virtue of its innate structure, is unable to differentiate perception from essence, or *res cogitans (interna) *from *res extensa (externa)*,it makes the naive assumption that it experiences and therefore knows ‘reality’, and that other viewpoints must therefore be ‘wrong’. For comfort and mental reinforcement, people tend to congregate with others who share the same paradigm. ## Temptations

Spiritual evolution is a process of unfoldment, emergence, and purification as a consequence of what one has become rather than as a result of what one is doing or has been. Spiritual errors can be concluded by humility and caution as well as forewarning. Temptations occur along the pathway and have been alluded to in the discussion of the various levels.
